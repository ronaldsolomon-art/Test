What the test proves — and doesn't. The re-optimized target kept landing on 40–50 (avg 44), and the win rate cleared 76% in all seven out-of-sample windows — that part is robust. Profitability is real on aggregate (+28.5%) but thin: two of seven windows were marginally negative. So this is a dependable high-win-rate strategy with a small edge, not a money printer. Figures are gross of slippage on mid prices, with unresolved trades excluded.
