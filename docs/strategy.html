EUR/USD · 15-minute · New York session

The Opening-Range Breakout, one trade a day

The first half hour sets a box. When price breaks out and closes past it, the strategy takes the move — aiming for a modest, reachable target that lands about 84% of the time.

  1. Mark the box

    The 09:30 and 09:45 New York candles set the opening range — its high and its low.

  2. Wait for the break

    After 10:00, the first candle to close above the box goes long; below it, short. One trade per day.

  3. Set both exits up front

    Target sits 40 points (~4 pips) past entry. The stop sits at the opposite side of the box.

  4. Let it resolve

    Price reaches the target (a win) or the stop (a loss). No new entries after 16:45.

Why the target is close and the stop is far: a nearby target is hit often, which is what lifts the win rate — but it only makes money because 40 points is big enough that the frequent wins outweigh the rarer full-box losses. Figures are from 5 years of real EUR/USD M15 data, gross of spread/slippage, validated out-of-sample and in the trailing year. Past performance is not a guarantee.
